Course Overview

This rigorous curriculum bridges theoretical statistics with practical public administration challenges. Participants will engage with real-world datasets derived from recent national surveys, learning to clean, process, and model data to extract actionable policy insights.

By the end of this module, learners will be proficient in utilizing predictive algorithms to assess the efficacy of resource allocation and demographic interventions.
